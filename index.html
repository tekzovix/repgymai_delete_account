Data Privacy

Request Account & Data Deletion

At RepGym AI (by Tekzovix), you have complete control over your personal account and workout data. You can delete your account and all associated fitness logs at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the RepGym AI app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom right icon).
  3. Tap Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, authentication credentials, workout history, custom splits, and AI chat logs will be deleted immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Within 30 Days)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual data erasure by contacting our support team:

  • Send an email to: tekzovix@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete My RepGym AI Account
  • Please include the email address associated with your RepGym AI account.

Upon verification, all stored database records and cloud backups will be permanently purged within 30 days.

Types of Data Deleted:

  • Personal profile details (Name, email address, profile picture).
  • All logged workouts, exercises, weights, personal records (PRs), and history.
  • Custom workout plans, splits, and questionnaires.
  • All AI Gym Coach conversation logs.
